\section*{Research}

\textbf{Senior Thesis} \hfill \textit{September 2025\,--\,April 2026}\\
I conducted a survey experiment using Qualtrics and Prolific to test how identity-based arguments and knowledge of India's no-first-use policy alter support for a hypothetical nuclear first strike. The project entailed writing survey instruments, constructing composite indices using inverse covariance weighting and principal component analysis, and running regression and interaction-effects models to analyze treatment heterogeneity. My thesis was awarded the Charles A.\,Beard Prize as the best paper in Columbia's political science department for the 2025--26 academic year. The final paper is available \href{https://wmk2114.github.io/my-site/pdfs/Senior%20Thesis%20Wyatt%20King.pdf}{here}.
