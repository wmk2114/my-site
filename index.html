Research & Sample Strategy @ Morning Consult

In July, I joined Morning Consult, where I work as an associate on its research and sample strategy team. There, I field surveys and think about how we can execute them better. One of the projects that I'm most proud of is building a dashboard that monitors incoming survey data, complementing that dashboard with question metadata and Qualtrics survey logic.

In May, I graduated Phi Beta Kappa and Magna cum Laude from Columbia University, where I studied international relations and statistics and covered the 2024 pro-Palestine protests as a photo and video journalist. My extracurriculars and internships spanned journalism, free speech advocacy, and data analytics—having worked at NBC News and PEN America, among other organizations.

One of my hobbies is reading, and I'm always on the hunt for new recommendations. I'm not sure how to describe my book taste, but my favorite authors are Kazuo Ishiguro, Herman Melville, and Virginia Woolf.
