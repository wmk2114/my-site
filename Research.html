My thesis was about state identity, institutionalized nuclear non-use, and public opinion. It asked how the norm against nuclear use can be strengthened, even as recent scholarship has shown Western public opinion to be far more receptive to nuclear use than previously thought. I am grateful to have received the Charles A. Beard Prize for this paper, given to the Columbia student who writes the best paper in political science during the academic year.
